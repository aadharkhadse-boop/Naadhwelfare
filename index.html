Who we are

A young foundation with a lasting purpose.

NAADH Welfare Foundation was incorporated on 20 June 2026 in Nagpur as a non-government, not-for-profit company dedicated to educational support services. We are new — and deliberately so. We are building carefully, listening to the families and schools around us, and designing programs that meet real needs rather than assumed ones.

Our name comes from नाद (naad) — resonance, the sound that carries. We believe a single opportunity to learn can echo through a child's life, their family, and their whole community. That belief guides everything we do.

The Foundation is led by its founding directors, Pradhnya Rajeshji Patil and Suyash Mukesh Bangde, who established NAADH to turn that conviction into steady, accountable work on the ground.

What we stand for

  • Equity in education. Support should reach the child who has the least, first.
  • Dignity, always. We work with communities as partners, never as recipients.
  • Accountability. Every rupee and every hour is used with care and transparency.
What we do

Focus areas we are building.

Our programs are designed around the everyday barriers that keep children out of learning. As a newly founded organisation, we are launching these in phases across Nagpur.

Learning support & tutoring

After-school and remedial tutoring for children who are falling behind — so no student is left to struggle alone with the basics of reading, writing and maths.

Scholarships & school-kit support

Fee assistance, books, uniforms and stationery for families for whom the cost of schooling is the single biggest hurdle to keeping a child enrolled.

Digital literacy

Hands-on computer and internet skills for students and young people, opening doors to modern learning, information and future employment.

Mentorship & guidance

Pairing students with mentors for career guidance, confidence and life skills — support that goes beyond the classroom toward a real future.

✦We're just getting started. These programs are rolling out in phases. If you'd like to help shape or fund one of them, we'd love to hear from you.
